### Mẫu 1 — Reddit (`r/EtsySellers`, `r/Etsy`) & Facebook Groups bán Etsy
**Tiêu đề:** `I broke down exact 2026 Etsy fees on a $34 + $5 shipping order (with vs. without 12%/15% Offsite Ads) — here is the quick math`
**Nội dung:**
> A lot of newer sellers price off the 6.5% transaction fee and wonder why payouts feel light. Here is the exact 2026 US breakdown on a **$34 item + $5 shipping ($39 gross)**:
> - Listing fee: **$0.20**
> - 6.5% transaction fee (applies to shipping too): **$2.54**
> - 3% + $0.25 Etsy Payments: **$1.42**
> - **Total organic Etsy fees:** **$4.16 (10.7%)**
> - **If Offsite Ads triggers (15%):** adds **$5.85** -> **$10.01 total fees (25.7%!)**
> - **If Mandatory 12% Offsite Ads ($10k+ shop):** adds **$4.68** -> **$8.84 total fees (22.7%)**
>
> Quick tip: If ~18% of your orders come from Offsite Ads, you don't need to raise prices a full 12%—a ~3% blended buffer across all listings covers the average hit without hurting conversion.
>
> If anyone wants to plug in their own COGS & shipping numbers (no signup/email required), I put together a free interactive calculator here: `https://shopmarginlab.com/etsy-offsite-ads-calculator/`
