### 1.1. `r/Depop` — Cảnh báo phí Boosted 12% + Free Ship trên sàn "0% selling fee"

- **Subreddit:** `https://www.reddit.com/r/Depop/submit`
- **Flair gợi ý:** `Selling` / `Discussion` / `Advice`
- **Tiêu đề:**
  `0% selling fees on Depop still turns into ~19–35% overhead if you stack Boosted + free shipping (quick math on a $38 tee)`
- **Nội dung (Copy-Paste):**
  > A lot of people see "0% selling fees" for US listings and assume they're keeping ~96% of the sale price, then get confused when their payout feels closer to Poshmark.
  >
  > Here is how a **$38 tee + $5.99 buyer-paid shipping** (with ~7% buyer sales tax) actually nets out with **$11 sourcing cost**:
  >
  > - **Organic Depop sale:** $0 selling fee, and Depop Payments (3.3% + $0.45) is charged on item + shipping + sales tax ($47.07 total), which comes out to **$2.00** (5.3% of the $38 item). Net profit = **$25.00** — you keep **$5.60 more** than Poshmark's $7.60 (20%) cut.
  > - **Sold via 12% Boosted Listing:** Boosted applies 12% to item + shipping ($43.99), adding **$5.28**. Total Depop fees jump to **$7.28** (19.2% of item price), dropping profit to **$19.72** — basically right back at Poshmark territory.
  > - **Boosted + you cover $5.99 free shipping:** Net profit drops to **$13.73** (36.1% margin instead of 65.8%).
  >
  > Practical takeaway from checking my own closet: never leave auto-boost turned on for brands that already search-sell organically (vintage tees, Carhartt, Y2K denim). Save the 12% boost for stale inventory sitting past 30 days, or pad the listing price ~12% before boosting.
  >
  > (If anyone wants to test a specific price/shipping/boost combo without making a spreadsheet, I put the 2026 formula in a no-login calculator here: https://shopmarginlab.com/depop-fee-calculator/)

### 1.2. `r/TikTokShopSellers` — Điểm hòa vốn hoa hồng Creator (~6.9% blended) so với Etsy

- **Subreddit:** `https://www.reddit.com/r/TikTokShopSellers/submit`
- **Tiêu đề:**
  `6% TikTok Shop referral fee vs. 15–20% creator commissions — the exact break-even math against Etsy`
- **Nội dung (Copy-Paste):**
  > Seeing a lot of sellers jump to TikTok Shop assuming the 6% referral fee automatically beats Etsy (~9.5% + $0.45 + occasional Offsite Ads), then realize cashflow feels tighter at the end of the month.
  >
  > Ran both side-by-side at **60 orders/month, $38 checkout total, $14.50 landed COGS + postage** ($2,280 monthly gross):
  >
  > - **Etsy US (assuming 15% of orders hit 15% Offsite Ads):** $243.60 base fees + $51.30 Offsite Ads = **$294.90 total fees (12.9% blended)** -> **$1,115.10/mo net**.
  > - **TikTok Shop (0% creator affiliate — all shop-owned video/LIVE):** $136.80 referral (6%) -> **$1,273.20/mo net** (+$158.10/mo ahead of Etsy).
  > - **TikTok Shop (12% blended creator cut across all orders):** $136.80 referral + $273.60 creator payouts = **$410.40 total fees (18.0%)** -> **$999.60/mo net** (Etsy beats it by $115.50/mo).
  >
  > The math break-even is roughly **6.9% blended creator cost** across total store revenue (e.g., ~45% of orders from 15%-commission creators and ~55% organic shop videos). If creators drive 80%+ of your volume at 15%–20%, single $20–$25 items get chewed up fast by $4.50 postage—bundling 2-packs above $40 is usually the cleanest fix.
  >
  > Put a quick interactive slider up if you want to plug in your own AOV and creator split (no signup): https://shopmarginlab.com/etsy-vs-tiktok-shop-calculator/

### 1.3. `r/EtsySellers` — So sánh thực tế Etsy vs. TikTok Shop cho chủ shop thủ công / POD

- **Tiêu đề:**

- **Nội dung (Copy-Paste):**
  > With everyone saying "TikTok Shop is only 6% fees," I wanted to see when moving catalog volume off Etsy actually saves money once you include TikTok creator affiliate cuts and Etsy Offsite Ads.
  >
  > Using **60 orders/month at $38 order total** ($14.50 COGS + postage):
  > - **Etsy US** (with 15% of orders attributed to 15% Offsite Ads) takes **$294.90/mo** (~12.9% blended), leaving **$1,115.10/mo net**.
  > - **TikTok Shop US** beats Etsy if you shoot your own organic videos/LIVE (**$1,273.20/mo** at 0% creator fee), or stay under ~6.9% blended creator commission across total sales.
  > - As soon as your shop leans on **15%–20% affiliate creators** for most orders (e.g. 12% blended across the shop = $410.40 fees), TikTok drops to **$999.60/mo** — $115.50/mo behind Etsy.
  >
  > Basically: TikTok Shop isn't cheaper if you're paying creators 15%+ to move $25 single items, and Etsy's search intent is still strong if you price in a ~2.5%–3% blended Offsite Ads buffer and nudge repeat buyers onto your own storefront later.
